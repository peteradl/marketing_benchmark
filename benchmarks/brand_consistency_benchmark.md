# Brand Consistency Benchmark v0.1

## Purpose

This benchmark evaluates whether an ADL artifact looks, sounds, and behaves consistently with `ADL Brand Guide v0.1`. It measures brand execution, not business-strategy alignment or content performance.

Use the separate Strategy Marketing Consistency Benchmark to test whether the artifact advances ADL's 2026 business strategy. Use analytics and channel-specific performance benchmarks separately.

## Evidence rule

Score only what is visible in the artifact and its directly linked destination. Do not infer compliance from the creator's intent or from internal information the audience cannot see.

For a campaign or series, score each artifact individually and the collection as a whole.

## Pre-score publication holds

Stop review and place the artifact on hold when it contains:

- confidential, protected, or nonpublic client information;
- an unsupported material claim, metric, funding amount, outcome, or partner role;
- an unlicensed image, font, logo, or other asset;
- materially incorrect client, partner, agency, or portfolio-company attribution;
- unreadable essential information that cannot be corrected through ordinary editing.

Resolve the hold before treating the numeric score as a publication decision.

## Scoring rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Logo system | 15 | Uses the correct approved logo variant, preserves proportions and clear space, meets minimum size, and has sufficient contrast. |
| Color system | 15 | Uses the approved palette by role, maintains restrained proportions, and reserves functional colors for meaning. |
| Typography | 15 | Uses Libre Franklin for headings and Roboto for body copy, with a clear, readable hierarchy and approved fallbacks where necessary. |
| Layout and hierarchy | 15 | Uses a consistent grid, alignment, spacing, information hierarchy, and one clear dominant message. |
| Imagery and graphics | 10 | Uses relevant, rights-cleared imagery or coherent diagrams, charts, and icons that reveal the actual system or subject. |
| Voice and terminology | 15 | Is systems-minded, evidence-led, plainspoken, outcome-oriented, collaborative, and consistent with approved ADL terminology. |
| Attribution and evidence | 5 | States ADL's role accurately, credits partners, labels projections, and provides sources for material claims. |
| Accessibility and production | 10 | Meets contrast, legibility, alt-text, captioning, responsive-fit, and technical-quality requirements appropriate to the surface. |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Dimension scoring levels

Assign one level to each dimension:

- **100% of weight:** fully compliant, specific, and polished.
- **75% of weight:** substantially compliant; minor correction or inconsistency remains.
- **50% of weight:** neutral or partially compliant; material improvement is available.
- **0% of weight:** materially inconsistent, missing where essential, or misleading.

Multiply the level by the dimension weight and round the final score to the nearest whole number.

## Rating bands

| Score | Rating | Decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 90-100 | Brand exemplary | Ready from a brand-consistency perspective after factual and approval checks. |
| 80-89 | Brand consistent | Publishable after minor corrections. |
| 65-79 | Revision recommended | Correct the highest-impact inconsistencies before publication. |
| 0-64 | Materially off brand | Rework the artifact using the guide and approved templates. |

## Override rules

The following rules override the numeric rating:

- **Altered or unapproved logo:** maximum rating is `Revision recommended` until replaced.
- **Distorted, cropped, or unreadable logo:** publication hold until corrected.
- **Primary typography replaced without a documented platform constraint:** maximum rating is `Revision recommended`.
- **Essential text below WCAG AA contrast or unreadable at the target size:** publication hold.
- **Color used as the only status signal:** maximum rating is `Revision recommended` until a label, icon, or text cue is added.
- **Generic consultancy positioning or a contradiction of the system-designer identity:** refer to the Strategy Marketing Consistency Benchmark; the artifact cannot be approved through this benchmark alone.
- **Unsupported claim, confidential information, or material role inflation:** publication hold regardless of score.

## Dimension guidance

### 1. Logo system - 15 points

Check:

- approved primary, stacked, symbol, or reverse use;
- correct proportions and colors;
- clear space;
- minimum size and legibility;
- background contrast;
- consistent placement across a series;
- no effects, recreation, or unauthorized co-branding.

### 2. Color system - 15 points

Check:

- core colors match approved hex values;
- accents guide attention rather than dominate;
- functional green, yellow, and red communicate status only;
- the artifact is not a one-note blue, teal, or green composition;
- text/background pairs meet contrast requirements;
- charts use consistent series colors and labels.

### 3. Typography - 15 points

Check:

- Libre Franklin headings and Roboto body;
- fallback fonts used only when the platform requires them;
- sentence-case headings;
- no negative letter spacing;
- readable sizes, line lengths, and line spacing;
- restrained use of bold, italics, all caps, and hierarchy levels.

### 4. Layout and hierarchy - 15 points

Check:

- visible grid and alignment;
- consistent margins and spacing;
- one dominant message;
- clear reading order;
- no overlapping, clipped, or crowded elements;
- no cards nested inside cards;
- titles fit their containers;
- visual emphasis matches content importance.

### 5. Imagery and graphics - 10 points

Check:

- real, inspectable subject matter rather than generic atmosphere;
- confirmed image rights;
- consistent icon or illustration family;
- diagrams clarify systems, flows, dependencies, or decisions;
- charts are labeled, sourced, and not misleading;
- partner and client logos are approved and proportionate.

### 6. Voice and terminology - 15 points

Check:

- direct, specific, and plainspoken writing;
- evidence and uncertainty are distinguished;
- focus on industry or audience outcomes;
- correct use of `Industrialized Construction`;
- ADL appears as a system designer rather than a conventional consultancy;
- limited jargon, superlatives, and generic transformation language;
- the tone is confident, collaborative, and not self-congratulatory.

### 7. Attribution and evidence - 5 points

Check:

- ADL's role is stated precisely;
- client, agency, partner, and portfolio contributions are credited;
- dates, figures, names, titles, funding, and claims are verified;
- projections and illustrative figures are labeled;
- data sources and observation dates are present when material.

### 8. Accessibility and production - 10 points

Check:

- WCAG AA contrast;
- alt text for informative images;
- captions or transcripts for meaningful audio/video;
- text fits and remains readable on mobile and desktop;
- links and controls have descriptive labels and visible focus states;
- export resolution, crop, margins, and file format are appropriate;
- the final artifact contains no comments, placeholders, drafting notes, or broken links.

## Surface-specific checks

### LinkedIn

- Evaluate the post copy, creative, thumbnail, and directly linked page.
- Confirm essential graphic text is legible on a mobile feed.
- Look for an approved logo, type hierarchy, and recognizable palette.
- Require relevant alt text when the platform supports it.
- Do not award full imagery points for generic stock photography with a logo overlay.

### Website

- Review desktop and mobile layouts.
- Check global logo, fonts, colors, buttons, links, image treatment, navigation, contrast, alt text, keyboard access, and focus states.
- Sample multiple pages before awarding full consistency points.

### Presentation or document

- Review the title page, representative content pages, charts, tables, imagery, and closing page.
- Confirm confidential markings and partner attribution where applicable.
- Check the final exported file, not only the editable source.

### Campaign or series

- Score individual assets and the series-level system.
- Full layout and logo points require consistent placement, hierarchy, and treatment across the collection.
