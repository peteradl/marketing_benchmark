# ADL Brand Surfaces

## Purpose

This register lists surfaces where audiences encounter the ADL Ventures brand. It includes public channels, ADL-operated initiatives, shared programs, recruiting, events, business materials, internal templates, and third-party profiles.

## Control definitions

| Control level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Owned | ADL controls the platform, content, and brand presentation. |
| Operated | ADL administers or substantially operates the surface under a program or initiative brand. |
| Shared | ADL shares governance, content, or approval with partners. |
| Influenced | ADL can supply or request changes to content but does not control publication. |
| Third-party | An external platform or publisher controls the surface. |
| Unverified | Ownership, access, activity, or current status was not confirmed. |
